# A practical enquiry workflow for your business

Sparrow Works can connect your enquiry forms to one organised workflow, check the information, spot repeat submissions and show which enquiries need attention.

Your team can review a proposed reply before it goes anywhere. If an integration fails or is rate-limited, the workflow keeps a record, retries where appropriate and flags anything that needs a person to investigate.

We would agree the forms, contact-matching rules, review process and follow-up wording with you, then test the full workflow and provide a clear handover. Real email or CRM connections would be added only with your approval.

Intake Rescue is our working n8n demonstration of that approach. It uses synthetic enquiries and a simulated delivery service; it is not presented as a client deployment or evidence of a particular financial result.
